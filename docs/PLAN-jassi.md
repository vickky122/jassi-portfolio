## 1. The big change: this is not a developer portfolio

The earlier plan assumed code projects, GitHub links and a tech stack. Jassi's proof of work is **internships, controls discipline, competitions and sport**. So:

| Developer plan | Jassi's version |
|---|---|
| Projects (problem / decision / result) | **Two internships as "case files"** (what I checked, against what, what could go wrong, evidence trail) |
| Skills as stat bars | **Skills as an honest status window**: no fake percentages, only "how I used it" |
| GitHub / demo links | Resume PDF (redacted), LinkedIn, email |
| Impressing with code | **Impressing with precision and clarity.** Audit recruiters reward accuracy and restraint, so the anime layer must never cost readability or credibility |

**Risk to design around:** audit and assurance is a conservative field. The anime treatment must be bold on the surface and strictly factual underneath. Two safety valves are mandatory: a **recap card** in the first screen (who, what, open to work) and a one-tap **plain view** (`/resume`, a clean one-page version). Anyone who just wants the facts bypasses the show.

## 2. Fit: why anime works for *this* story

- **Audit is detective work.** "Verify entries against source records", "a single incorrect field can hold a consignment", "the documentation trail that evidences each step." The case-file arc format turns his own CV lines into the plot.
- **His sport record is a sports-anime arc.** State-level badminton (Odisha) and football (Rajasthan), squash silver, 3rd at NIT Rourkela two years running, team lead to Runners-Up. That becomes a "tournament arc", and it supports teamwork and leadership.
- **Languages = "audio tracks":** `audio: english · hindi · odia · french`, as the dual-audio switch on a streaming player. Small, charming, true.
- **The CV photo shows a dark suit with a red-striped tie**, which already matches the crimson/black palette. The character can wear it: a sharp, formal anime portrait, not a streetwear one.

### Case-file template (Arcs 1 and 2)
`the assignment` → `what I checked, and against what` → `why it matters (the control)` → `evidence trail` → `what I learned`. Result lines are qualitative until real numbers exist (see gaps). Example, using only CV wording:

- **Arc 1: payroll (HR intern, payroll department, May-Jun 2024).** Supported monthly payroll for Sales and Marketing within a defined approval and review process; cross-checked compensation inputs against source data before release; exposure to statutory deductions, payroll compliance and the documentation trail. *Control lesson:* why access restriction and review exist.
- **Arc 2: exports and logistics (intern, Jun-Aug 2026).** Prepared and checked export documentation against the underlying paperwork ("a manual control where a single incorrect field can hold a consignment"); coordinated with internal teams and logistics partners; tracked consignments and escalated exceptions against committed timelines.
- **Arc 3: tournament arc.** Best Brand Presentation Award (Bizz Bash, Amity Noida, team); Team Lead, HR Conclave, Runners-Up (inter-college); 3rd at the NIT Rourkela inter-university competition, 2024 and 2025; state-level badminton and football; squash silver (Amity Sangathan, 2025).
